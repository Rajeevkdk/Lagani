# A live investing product is a separate project

The simulation does not establish legal or operational readiness for public equity investment in Nepal. Adding a payment gateway would not resolve these gaps.

- Obtain qualified Nepal-specific review of the operating entity, securities offering model, solicitation, investor eligibility and required authorizations.
- Define actual investor rights, shareholder agreements, disclosures, dilution, governance, complaints, exit constraints and corporate records.
- Establish identity/beneficial-owner verification, sanctions and applicable financial-crime controls with appropriate providers and accountable human operations.
- Establish custody, segregated funds, payment reconciliation, refunds, settlement and insolvency treatment with approved institutions.
- Define tax, investor reporting, company reporting and record-retention obligations from verified advice.
- Perform independent security, privacy and financial-control review, with incident response, recovery drills, access reviews and fraud monitoring.
- Validate real financial evidence and research quality with honest provenance and qualified reviewers. Demo fixtures do not validate underwriting or predictive accuracy.
- Obtain separately authorized hosting, provider contracts and store submissions; complete required account verification and genuine testing participation.

Keep simulated balances, certificates and histories isolated from any future real-money system. They must never be converted into actual investment positions.
